## Video direction

- Palette system: use `frame.md` literally—ink-black/graphite is the dominant register, cream carries primary copy, and verified mint is the single accent. Amber and red appear only when the product state itself is pending or blocked.
- Type system: Archivo display establishes one dominant statement; body copy stays restrained; IBM Plex Mono carries hashes, model names, run IDs, storage keys, labels, and counters.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail settles, VO-paced sequential reveals, and velocity-matched internal seams. At time zero, show only the first spoken cue; hold later evidence until it is named, especially through the back half.
- Camera grammar: every move must connect evidence. Frames 2, 6, 8, and 10 get one motivated surface or camera journey; comparison and ledger frames stay locked so the proof can be read.
- Rhythm: Frames 5, 7, and 11 are deliberate evidence holds after their decisive state lands. Frame 12 is the calmest held close.
- Caption keep-out: all load-bearing content stays in the upper 83% of the canvas; the caption band remains clear.
- Negative list: no generic AI gradients, fake browser chrome, decorative dashboards, floating bokeh, invented metrics, bounce-first motion, lazy breathing, back-half drift, slideshow front-loading, or screensaver-style independent motion.
